Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At BettingClarity, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our Decision Management System.

1. Important Disclaimer

  1. BettingClarity is not a gambling operator, bookmaker, or financial advisory service.
  2. All data processed is user-submitted and used strictly for educational, analytical, and productivity purposes.
  3. We do not provide betting advice or outcome predictions.

2. Information We Collect

  1. Personal Information: We collect only the information necessary to create and manage your account, such as your email address and subscription status. Payments are processed via Stripe.
  2. User-Generated Content (Betting Data): To deliver core features, we process data voluntarily entered into the Decision Journal, including:
    1. Betting history (selections, stakes, odds)
    2. Psychological indicators (emotional state, confidence)
    3. Timing and decision rationale

3. How We Use Your Information

  1. Service Provision: We use your data to generate personalized reports such as Leak Detection, Psych Audits, and strategy analysis.

4. Data Security

  1. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.
  2. Payment transactions are handled by Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor.

5. Cookies

  1. We use cookies to maintain your login session and ensure proper platform functionality.
  2. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

  1. Access and Correction: You can view and edit your logged data within the Decision Journal and Reports sections.
  2. Account Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated data by contacting support@bettingclarity.com.
